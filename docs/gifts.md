# Gifts and drip bags

## Drip bags

A drip bag is a filter pouch of ground coffee with paper hooks that hang over a cup. Pour hot water slowly in two or three stages, wait, and remove the bag. They need no equipment, which makes them handy for offices, travel and gift boxes. See [Caffeine Brothers drip bags](https://caffeinebrothers.co/product-category/all-products/coffee-drip-bags/).
