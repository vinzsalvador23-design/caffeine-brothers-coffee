# Caffeine Brothers Coffee Guide

This guide covers the basics of buying and brewing Philippine coffee: the main varieties, how to compare prices, what to look for in a supplier, and which formats suit gifts and offices.

It is maintained by [Caffeine Brothers](https://caffeinebrothers.co/), a coffee beans supplier in Makati City, Philippines.

## Sections

- [Philippine coffee varieties](varieties.md): Arabica, Robusta, Liberica (Kapeng Barako) and Excelsa
- [Buying for a cafe or office](buying-for-business.md): suppliers, price per kilo and startup costs
- [Gifts and drip bags](gifts.md): single-serve drip bags and Christmas gift sets
