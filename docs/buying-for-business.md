# Buying coffee for a cafe or office

## Compare prices per kilo

Bags come in many sizes, so convert everything to price per kilo before comparing. Compare the same variety and roast, and check the roast date. The [Caffeine Brothers coffee beans price tracker](https://caffeinebrothers.co/coffee-beans-price-tracker-philippines/) lists current prices per kilo in the Philippines.

## What to ask a supplier

- Which varieties and origins do you carry?
- How fresh is the roast on delivery?
- Can you grind for my equipment, or supply whole bean?
- What are the minimum order and delivery schedule?
- Can I try a sample first?

[Caffeine Brothers wholesale](https://caffeinebrothers.co/wholesale/) supplies cafes, restaurants, hotels and offices, with free samples for businesses and nationwide delivery.

## Planning a new coffee business

If you are starting a coffee cart, kiosk or pop-up, work out your costs before buying equipment. The [Caffeine Brothers pop-up coffee shop cost calculator](https://caffeinebrothers.co/pop-up-coffee-shop-cost-calculator/) estimates startup costs and break-even from your own volume and coffee type.
